Delete your Tucker Tracker account

Tucker Tracker is developed by xCalibre-AI. This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data when you do.

Option 1: delete your account directly in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tucker Tracker and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Manage account.
  4. Tap Delete my account.
  5. Re-enter your password to confirm (this is a safeguard, since deletion can't be undone).

Your account is deleted immediately once you confirm — there's no waiting period.

Option 2: request deletion without opening the app

If you don't have the app installed, or can't sign in, email wumasterclaw@gmail.com from the email address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my Tucker Tracker account." We'll delete your account and confirm once it's done.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently deletes, immediately (not after a delay, and not just hidden from view):

  • your account and profile information (name, email, daily energy target)
  • your entire food diary — every logged entry, on every day
  • your units preference and other in-app settings

This is enforced at the database level (deleting the account cascades to delete this data automatically), not just removed from what you can see.

What isn't deleted

Any custom food you created that's still referenced by another user's diary entry, or that exists independently of your account, stays in the shared food catalog — but it's anonymised: it's no longer linked to your account or identifiable as yours in any way.

We don't currently retain any of your data for a fixed period after deletion — it's gone as soon as the deletion completes.
